ResumeHack Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

ResumeHack ("we", "our", or "the extension") is an open-source AI Career & Resume Copilot designed to assist users in optimizing, tailoring, and formatting their resumes for specific job descriptions directly within Google Docs.

We believe your career data and resume content belong solely to you. This Privacy Policy details the exact data accessed, processed, transmitted, and stored when you use ResumeHack.

1. Google User Data Accessed & Permissions

ResumeHack requests access to specific Google API scopes strictly to provide document tailoring and PDF export functionality:

  • Google Docs API (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/documents): Used to read structural paragraphs and bullet points from your designated resume document to calculate ATS benchmark scores and generate STAR tailoring suggestions. When you explicitly click "Apply" or "Accept", ResumeHack uses documents.batchUpdate to write the optimized bullet text directly into your Google Doc.
  • Google Drive API (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file): Access is strictly restricted under the narrow drive.file scope. ResumeHack only accesses files that you explicitly select using the Google Drive Picker modal or files created directly by the application (e.g., duplicated tailored copies). ResumeHack does not have access to your broader Google Drive files, folders, or unauthorized documents. This permission is used solely to fetch the selected resume, fork company-specific copies (files.copy), and export PDF binaries (files.export).
  • User Profile Email (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email): Used solely to display your connected Google account email in the extension's Settings tab so you can confirm which account is actively linked.

🔒 Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

ResumeHack's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

2. Third-Party Data Transmission & AI Processing

To generate resume tailoring suggestions and ATS scoring analyses:

  • Resume and Job Description Text Transmission: When you initiate an ATS audit or tailoring request, the text of your selected resume bullet points and the job description from your active browser tab are transmitted to the user-configured AI model provider (such as the Google Gemini API, Anthropic Claude API, or a locally-hosted Ollama model).
  • Purpose Limitation: This data is processed transiently and exclusively to generate STAR bullet rewrites, extract relevant technical keywords, and identify missing competencies.
  • No Model Training: Your resume text and personal information are not used to train or fine-tune third-party foundation AI models.

3. Data Storage & Local Security Architecture

ResumeHack is built with a local-first privacy architecture:

  • Client-Side Storage (chrome.storage.local): OAuth access tokens, refresh tokens, user preferences (such as target job title and candidate name), and tailor history are stored locally in your browser's isolated extension storage. Tokens are never uploaded to any external database or third-party server.
  • Stateless Backend Proxy: The ResumeHack backend service (/api/auth/google/exchange) operates strictly as a stateless token exchange proxy to keep OAuth client secrets secure without bundling secrets in the extension. The backend server does not persist, database-log, or store your resume content, job listings, or OAuth tokens on disk.

4. Data Sharing & Monetization

  • No Sale of Data: ResumeHack does not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data, resume content, or Google credentials to data brokers, advertisers, or third parties.
  • No Tracking: ResumeHack does not track your browsing history outside of extracting job postings from user-navigated career pages (e.g. LinkedIn, Greenhouse, Lever) when you interact with the extension.

5. User Control & Data Revocation

You have full control over your data and connected authorizations at any time:

  • Disconnect Account: You can disconnect your Google account and remove all stored tokens instantly by clicking "Disconnect Account" in the extension's Settings tab.
  • Revoke Google Access: You can revoke ResumeHack's access from your Google Account at any time via Google Account Third-Party Access Settings.
  • Clear Extension Data: Uninstalling the extension or clearing extension data immediately deletes all locally saved preferences and tokens from your machine.
