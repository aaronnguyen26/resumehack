ResumeHack

AI Career & Resume Copilot

ResumeHack is an AI Career & Resume Copilot that tailors your resume to job postings and applies suggestions directly in Google Docs.

📝 Direct Google Docs Apply

Applies tailored STAR bullet optimizations and keyword enhancements directly to your Google Doc with atomic reverse-index batch updates.

🔒 Scoped Drive Integration

Built with Google's strict drive.file scope and Google Drive Picker, accessing only documents you explicitly choose.

🎯 ATS Rubric Scoring

Deterministic 7-factor scoring inspired by engineering evaluation rubrics—analyzing production experience, projects, metrics, and verbs.

🛡️ Local Privacy First

OAuth tokens are stored locally on your device in browser storage. Your resume text is never permanently stored on proxy servers.
