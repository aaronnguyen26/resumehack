About ResumeHack

ResumeHack is a browser extension designed to help job seekers tailor and optimize their resumes for specific job descriptions. When browsing job postings on platforms like LinkedIn, Greenhouse, or Lever, ResumeHack extracts the job requirements and compares them against your master resume. It evaluates key competencies, computes Applicant Tracking System (ATS) benchmark match scores, generates tailored STAR-method bullet-point suggestions, and applies user-accepted edits directly into your Google Doc in real time.

🔐 Google Account Access & Permissions

ResumeHack requests limited Google Account permissions exclusively to enable document inspection, live resume editing, and PDF exports:

  • Google Docs API (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/documents): Required to read the structural text and paragraphs of your master resume for ATS evaluation, and to write accepted bullet improvements and keyword adjustments directly into your Google Doc using atomic batch updates.
  • Google Drive API (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file): Strictly restricted to files you explicitly select via the Google Drive Picker modal or files created by the application (e.g. duplicated tailored copies). ResumeHack does not request or possess broad access to your Google Drive files or folders. This permission is used solely to locate your chosen resume document, duplicate company-specific copies, and export tailored PDF versions.
  • User Identity (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email): Used solely to display your connected Google account email in the extension's Settings panel for user confirmation.
🛡️ Limited Use Disclosure: ResumeHack's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Core Features

📝 Direct Google Docs Apply

Applies tailored STAR bullet improvements and keyword additions directly into your Google Doc using atomic reverse-index batch updates.

🔒 Scoped Drive Integration

Employs Google's secure drive.file scope and Google Drive Picker, accessing only the documents you explicitly authorize.

🎯 7-Category ATS Scoring

Deterministic rubric analysis evaluating production experience, personal projects, hard technical skills, metrics, and power verbs.

🛡️ Local-First Privacy

OAuth tokens and candidate preferences remain stored locally on your device in chrome.storage.local. Documents are never stored on external proxy servers.
